Faster Convergence

The red curve rises sharply within the first few hundred RL steps, while the Action-Only+PPO baseline takes several times longer to reach the same level — and never catches up on LIBERO-Spatial or LIBERO-Long.

Higher Final Accuracy

At convergence, $\text{LaST}_0^*$+LAPO asymptotes near 100% success on every suite, beating the baseline by a wide margin — most strikingly on the long-horizon suite (99.4% vs. 88.2%).

Stronger OOD Transfer

On held-out tasks the policy reaches 100% OOD success on Spatial-Task0, Object-Task0, and Goal-Task5 — while the Action-Only+PPO baseline overfits to training kinematics and even degrades on long-horizon tasks.

Both axes — sample efficiency and asymptotic performance — improve simultaneously, indicating that latent reasoning is not just a regularizer but a fundamentally better optimization substrate for VLA policies.

Real-World Experiments

We deploy $\text{LaST}_0^*$ on Franka Research 3 hardware across four manipulation tasks — Insert hexagon block, Open bag zipper (dual-arm), Insert USB into port, and Open marker cap. Each task uses only 20 expert demonstrations for warm-up, followed by online RL post-training with LoRA. Every task is evaluated under three out-of-distribution perturbations (Object / Background / Lighting) to stress-test generalization.

Abstract

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models have increasingly incorporated reasoning mechanisms for complex robotic manipulation. While explicit linguistic reasoning offers structured guidance, it suffers from inference latency and discretization bottlenecks. Continuous latent reasoning provides a more expressive alternative, yet existing approaches remain grounded in imitation learning, limiting adaptability and generalization. Although online reinforcement learning (RL) enables exploration through trial-and-error, it is largely restricted to vanilla action-space optimization, bypassing the underlying physical reasoning process.

We present $\text{LaST}_0^*$, a unified VLA framework that integrates latent Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning over physical dynamics prior to action execution, along with a tailored RL post-training paradigm. We propose Latent-to-Action Policy Optimization (LAPO), a novel RL algorithm that jointly optimizes the latent reasoning process and the action policy. Furthermore, an adaptive latent CoT mechanism allows the policy to dynamically adjust its reasoning horizon based on environment complexity.

Extensive experiments show that $\text{LaST}_0^*$ achieves a near-perfect 99.8% average success rate on LIBERO with only one-shot supervised warm-up. In real-world deployments, LAPO post-training yields up to a 50% improvement over the SFT policy across four complex tasks, including both single-arm and dual-arm settings.
